## Abstract

**Canonical liftings** were introduced [in a recent pre-print](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/1348) as a means to compress group-action-based sigma protocols. Intuitively, they generalise existing techniques for reducing the size of group-action-based signatures, such as the canonical forms used in the LESS. They are inherently a compression argument; however, under some hypotheses, they seem to preserve the hardness of the underlying group-action problem. 

They were used to produce a (linkable) ring signature, CE(Le)ReS. Can they be used to construct other primitives, such as blind signatures? What happens if we instantiate them on, e.g., the group actions underlying lattices and isogenies?
